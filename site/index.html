Open the app

Your resume, in your browser.

Open Vitae is a free resume builder that keeps everything on your device. No account, no server, no tracking. Write it, lay it out on real pages, and export it to PDF, Word or anything else you need.

Start writing Source on GitHub

  • Free software, GPL-3.0
  • Left-to-right and right-to-left
  • Works offline

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser. Resumes, photos, fonts and every saved version stay on your device. The app has no backend, no analytics and no cookies.
Every save is a version. Each save is kept with its date and time, so you can go back to any earlier state.
Yours to take away. Export a full JSON backup at any time and import it anywhere Open Vitae runs.

Everything a resume needs

  • Real pages

    A4, US Letter and every common paper size, smart or manual page breaks, and an optional header and footer with page numbers.

  • Export anywhere

    PDF with clickable links, Word, InDesign, PNG, JPG and SVG at up to 600 DPI, and JSON, Excel, CSV and Markdown.

  • Import and templates

    Bring a resume in from JSON, Excel, CSV or Markdown, or fill in a downloadable template.

  • A full text editor

    Lists, tables, links, colours, find and replace, and a format painter.

  • Drag to arrange

    Reorder sections and entries with the mouse, touch or the keyboard.

  • Fonts and photos

    Bundled, system, Google or your own fonts; crop your photo with a print-size guide.

  • Installable

    Install it like an app. It works offline and asks before updating.

  • Grammar, privately

    Optional grammar checking with a LanguageTool server you run yourself.

A closer look

Built for every direction

Right-to-left resumes are first-class, not an afterthought. Write in Persian, Arabic or Kurdish with mixed English terms, choose Western, Arabic-Indic or Persian digits, and write dates in the Gregorian, Persian, Islamic or Kurdish calendar.

The app itself speaks English, فارسی, العربية and کوردی.

The name and the mark

Curriculum vitae means “the course of a life”. The logo joins two Kurdish signs. The chin chevron of deq, the traditional Kurdish tattoo, is also the V of Vitae. Roj, the 21-ray sun of the flag of Kurdistan, shines in its opening, where the sign would have its dot.

A deq has been described as a diary of the life of the person who wears it; a resume is the same story, written for the next step. Read the story.

Sibling projects: Roj, Zimanyar, Zimankit, Tajdin and Deq.

Run it yourself

Open Vitae is a static site. Host it anywhere, or run the hardened container (read-only, non-root, no capabilities): 

git clone https://github.com/kanilabs/open-vitae.git
cd open-vitae
docker compose up --build        # http://127.0.0.1:8080

Add --profile grammar for a private LanguageTool server. See the documentation.
